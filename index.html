The Brand Examiner

One brand. One decision. One verdict.

The Brand Examiner is a documentary channel that performs autopsies on American consumer brands: what killed them, what saved them, and what quietly made them more expensive. Every case is built from public filings, court records, and reputable reporting.

Brand Examiner Pipeline

Brand Examiner Pipeline is a private production tool used only by the channel operator. It uploads the channel's own draft videos, audio, and images to the channel's own Google Drive for review, and manages the channel's own YouTube uploads and analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
